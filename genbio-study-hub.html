Energy Transformation

ATP, Cellular Respiration, Fermentation, and Photosynthesis

ATP ADP Cycle

ATP is the cell's energy currency. Hydrolysis removes a phosphate: ATP + H2O → ADP + Pi + energy.

Cellular Respiration order

Glycolysis›Pyruvate oxidation›Krebs Cycle›ETC + Chemiosmosis

Glycolysis

Location: cytosol
Net: 2 pyruvate, 2 ATP, 2 NADH
Cue: invest → split → payoff

Krebs Cycle per turn

Acetyl-CoA enters; oxaloacetate is regenerated. Produces 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 ATP or GTP, 2 CO2. No NADPH.

ETC and Chemiosmosis

NADH and FADH2 donate electrons. H+ is pumped, then flows through ATP synthase. Oxygen is the final electron acceptor and forms H2O.

Fermentation

Main purpose: regenerate NAD+ so Glycolysis continues. Types: Lactic Acid Fermentation and Alcohol Fermentation.

Photosynthesis

6CO2 + 6H2O + light → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Two stages

Light Dependent Reactions: thylakoid membrane; make ATP, NADPH, O2.
Calvin Cycle: stroma; uses CO2, ATP, NADPH to make G3P.

Plant Morphology and Physiology

Water balance, specialized structures, transport, nutrients, hormones

Plant cell water response

Hypotonic: turgid
Isotonic: flaccid
Hypertonic: plasmolysis

Specialized plant parts

  • Root hairs: absorption area
  • Root nodules: nitrogen-fixing bacteria
  • Rhizome: starch and protein storage
  • Prop roots: anchor and support

Xylem

Dead, lignified cells. Moves water and minerals mainly upward. Also provides support.

Phloem

Living sieve tubes with companion cells. Moves products of Photosynthesis from source to sink; bidirectional.

Essential element anchors

Mg: chlorophyll
N: growth and chlorophyll
P: ATP and nucleic acids
K: stomata and enzymes
Ca: cell wall and membrane

Hormone anchors

ABA: stress and stomata
Auxin: cell elongation
CK: cell differentiation
ET: ripening and defense
GA: elongation and germination
JA/SA: defense

Monocot vs dicot stem

Monocot: vascular bundles scattered.
Dicot: vascular bundles arranged in a ring.

Biotechnology

Agrobacterium tumefaciens transfers DNA. Multigene transfer introduces larger constructs. Genome editing deletes, mutates, or integrates a target gene.

Animal Morphology and Physiology

Reproduction, gametes, hormones, fertilization, and development

Asexual Reproduction

Regeneration: replace or form body parts
Budding: outgrowth from parent
Parthenogenesis: unfertilized egg develops

Sexual Reproduction

Haploid gametes combine to form a diploid zygote. Isogamy uses similar gametes; Anisogamy uses different gametes.

Gametogenesis

Spermatogenesis: 4 functional sperm.
Oogenesis: 1 functional ovum plus polar bodies.

Hormone control

FSH: sperm production or follicle development.
LH: testosterone production or Ovulation.
Estrogen: endometrium thickening.
Progesterone: maintains endometrium.

Development sequence

Zygote›Cleavage›Morula›Blastula›Gastrulation›Organogenesis

Three germ layers

Ectoderm: epidermis and nervous system
Endoderm: digestive and respiratory linings
Mesoderm: muscle, skeleton, circulation, urogenital system

Extraembryonic membranes

Yolk sac: nutrients/early blood cells
Amnion: fluid protection
Chorion: exchange/placenta
Allantois: wastes/umbilical vessels

Birth

Dilation → Expulsion → Delivery of placenta. Labor uses positive feedback: stretching increases contractions, causing more stretching.
